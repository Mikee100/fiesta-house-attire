About Us

316 Upperhill chambers is a modern grade A office tower that sets new standards in office design and ambience. It is located on 2nd Ngong Avenue approximately 2km from the CBD in the heart of Nairobi’s new financial hub. This is within close proximity to Milimani law court; various government ministries; statutory bodies and medical institutions.

The tower has 26 floors consisting of 3 basement parking floors, ground floor, mezzanine floor, 5 upper parking floors, 15 typical office floors and five penthouses. Efficiency in motor traffic is achieved by entry being from 2nd Ngong Avenue and exit through 3rd Ngong avenue.

On the ground floor is a stunning double volume entrance lobby with a states of the art access control. The inspiring modern tower is designed with banking hall on the ground floor and flexible office units ideal for medium to large office users on the upper floors. In addition, situate at the roof terrace are penthouses which command breath taking panoramic views of the city and its environs. These penthouses are available for rent on a short-term or long-term basis.

As a trendsetter of innovation, 316 Upperhill chambers has a state of art mechanical parking system. The parking system has been installed in the basement. There is provision for additional mechanical parking in the other parking floors. Currently there are 764 parking bays, an adequate ratio to the rentable area. There are several secure storage spaces available to the tenants or clients who require storage for their sensitive files and data.

Amenities

  • 316 Upperhill Chambers is served by 6 high speed intelligent lifts.

  • The façade is a unitized system which is a new innovation in the city. Inter alia, system radically reduces external noises, heat and glare from sunlight.

  • The tower uses solar power as an integral source of energy. This greatly reduce reliance on electric power; thus contributing to the green environment. In addition, adequate standby generators have been installed.

  • There is a state of art security system, which includes access control, visitor management and CCTV systems.

  • There is adequate water storage facilities that are also supplemented by water from the borehole. In addition, provision has been made for harvesting rain water for general cleaning and landscaping.

  • As a grade A office tower, provision has been made for s tenant to be able to install air conditioning if so desired.

There are 540 parking spaces at 316 Upperhill Chambers, a sufficient ratio of parking space to offices available.

Facilities

Parking

As a trendsetter of innovation ,316 Upperhill chambers boasts a new comprehensive state of art mechanical parking system designed to ensure convenient and secure parking. The parking system has been installed in one basement parking floor.

Currently there are a total of 540 parking bays, an adequate ratio to the rentable space. There is provision for additional mechanical parking in the upper floors if the need arises.

Storage Space

There are various storage areas in the building available for rent to tenants who need storage for sensitive files and data. Our storage facilities are well-protected, with 24 hours security including CCTV surveillance. We offer a variety of storage space sizes depending on what the tenants are looking for.

Gym

The gym is one of the best in the area, equipped with a variety of cardio machines, weight training equipment, aerobics etc. There are adequate changing rooms and lockers. The gym is available for use by the tenants at nominal fee.

High end restaurant

Alan Bobbe's Bistro, a renowned franchise is located on the mezzanine floor and is approximately 6135 square feet in size. It is designed to have a coffee shop; main dining area; bar; VIP dining rooms and a large terrace with cocktail lounge.

The restaurant has an open concept with an amazing ambiance. It is available for catered events and business meetings.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How many floors does the 316 Upperhill Chambers have?

    The 316 Upperill Chambers tower has 26 floors, 8 parking floors, ground floor, mezzanine, 15 office floors and penthouses.

  • Is there any hotel or restaurant where someone can eat or drink?

    Yes, on the Mezzanine floor, you will find Alan Bobbe's bistro restaurant. The restaurant has a coffee area, a bar area, an event terrace, and VIP rooms.

  • Does the building has residential apartments?

    Yes, there are five executive penthouses with unmatched panoramic views on the 21st floor. These penthouses are all furnished and serviced.

  • As a visitor can I get parking space at 316 Upperhill chambers?

    Yes, there is a parking floor specifically for serving visitors at a reasonable fee and there is enough of parking space.

  • How can I get to 316 Uppehill Chambers?

    The building's entrance is on 2nd Ngong Avenue and its exit is on 3rd Ngong Avenue. This ensures a smooth flow of traffic in and out.

  • Which organizations are based in 316 Upperhill Chambers?

    The building is home to a number of institutions, including NTSA, Business Registration Services, Letshego, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Lapfund, National Land Commission, Ngatia & Associates Advocates and Allan Bobbe's bistro restaurants.
